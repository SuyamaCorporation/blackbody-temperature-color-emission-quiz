Key Laws

Wien's Displacement Law
λ_max = b / T
b = 2.898×10⁻³ m·K (Wien constant)
T = temperature in Kelvin
λ_max = peak wavelength in meters
Stefan-Boltzmann Law
P = σ A T⁴
σ = 5.67×10⁻⁸ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴
A = surface area · ε (emissivity)
Power scales as T to the 4th power
Color-Temperature Guide
~1000 K — deep red glow
~3000 K — orange/warm white (incandescent)
~5778 K — yellow-white (Sun)
~10000 K — blue-white (A-type stars)
~30000 K+ — ultraviolet dominant
